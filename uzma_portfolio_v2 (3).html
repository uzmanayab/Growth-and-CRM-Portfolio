About Me

I am a Campaign & Growth Analyst at Prosperr.io, specializing in lifecycle marketing, CRM campaign management, and performance analytics for B2C and B2B audiences. With hands-on experience running multi-channel campaigns across Google Ads, Influencer Marketing, WhatsApp, Email, and Push, I connect data to decisions.
